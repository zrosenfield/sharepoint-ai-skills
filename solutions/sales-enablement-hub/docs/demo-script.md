# Demo script (8 minutes)

The story: a seller has a QBR with a retail customer tomorrow. The content team has to trust what gets shown.

**Before you start:**

- Sign in as an owner who is also the approver.
- Have one Content Intake submission un-reviewed.
- Open Home in one tab and Content Intake in another.

| # | Show | Say | Prompt or click |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | **Home** | "One place for everything a seller needs: approved content, the plays that work, and a deck in minutes. It's all SharePoint." | Scroll: featured plays, popular content |
| 2 | **Find** | "Only approved, current content shows up, tagged by autofill." | Filter Industry = Retail, then Type = Customer story |
| 3 | **Plays** | "Each play gives the pitch, why now, discovery questions and objections, plus the assets to use." | Open *Land with data, expand with AI* |
| 4 | Menu **Build a deck** | "Pick the meeting you're walking into." | **Quarterly business review** > **Build with this template** |
| 5 | Copilot card 1 | "Two questions, not a form." | Retail · Wingtip Retail · "Expand self-service analytics to all regional offices next quarter" |
| 6 | Copilot card 2 | "It only offers approved slides. Internal-only and expired ones never appear." | Accept the recommendations |
| 7 | **Decks > Start here** | "The deck, and what to say: an opening line, the new title and a line per slide." | Open the deck and the Talking points |
| 8 | **Content Intake** | "Now the other side: how content gets in." | Select the submission > **Run agent review** |
| 9 | Agent result | "The agent checked our ten publishing rules, fixed what was safe as a new version, and told the submitter what's left." | Show Validation findings and Agent fixes |
| 10 | **Submit for approval**, then approve | "One human decision, and only after the agent passes it." | Approve in the approval pane |
| 11 | **Publish to library** | "One click and it's live for every seller." | Show it in Find |
| 12 | Content Library > **Share with a customer** | "Sharing is governed too: specific-people view links only, and every share is logged." | Share a customer-shareable asset, then open Customer Shares |

**If time allows:**

- Content Library > **Check freshness**: shows the expired and expiring assets and the shares that depend on them.
- Publishing Desk: the content team's queue.

**Fallbacks:**

- If the Copilot cards are slow, open the finished sample deck in Decks.
- If the agent review takes too long, show a pre-reviewed submission.

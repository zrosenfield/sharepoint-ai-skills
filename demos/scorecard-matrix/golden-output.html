The clear recommendation: top Ease of Use score (5/5) delivers the fastest path to rep adoption, while strong marks across Integration, Pricing, and Support make it the most balanced scorecard in the evaluation. Customization depth is the only relative weakness — not a blocker at Zava's current stage.

Salesforce 3.85 / 5.00

Exceptional on Integration (5/5), Customization (5/5), and Data Privacy (5/5) — the most capable platform evaluated. A single weak score on Pricing (2/5, the highest-TCO option) costs 0.50 points under Zava's weighting and keeps it in second place; revisit if integration depth becomes the primary constraint at Series C scale.

3rd

The dark horse: strong across Ease of Use, Integration, Pricing, and Privacy with no critical weaknesses. The newer CSM ecosystem (Support: 3/5) is the main risk — if Freshworks's enterprise support matures, this option closes the gap on HubSpot at lower cost.

Scoring Methodology

Dimension weights and assessment criteria · 1–5 scale · 1 = very poor fit, 3 = adequate, 5 = exceptional
Ease of Use 20% Onboarding speed, UI clarity, rep adoption — time-to-value for the sales team
Integration Depth 25% Native connectors to Zava Ops systems, Slack, billing platform, and email/calendar
Pricing Fit 20% Total cost at 50-seat enterprise plan over 3 years, including implementation and add-ons
Support Quality 15% Response time SLA, availability of a dedicated customer success manager, documentation depth
Customization 10% Custom fields, workflow automation builder, and public API access for future integrations
Data Privacy 10% SOC 2 Type II certification, GDPR compliance posture, and EU/US data residency options
